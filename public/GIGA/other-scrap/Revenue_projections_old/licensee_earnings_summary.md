## KEY SUCCESS FACTORS FOR LICENSEES

### ✅ You Should License If:
- Your gross margins are 25%+ (preferably 30%+)
- You have an established customer base or distribution
- The IP gives you significant competitive advantage
- You can achieve $1M+ in revenue within 2 years
- Your business model is service-based or high-margin

### ❌ Licensing May Not Work If:
- Your gross margins are below 20%
- You're in early startup phase with no revenue
- The 15% royalty would make you unprofitable
- You can't scale to at least $1M revenue
- You're in a commoditized, low-margin industry

## PAYMENT STRUCTURE

### How the 15% Royalty Works

1. **Calculation:** Based on **gross revenue** from products/services using the IP
2. **Frequency:** Quarterly payments typical

5. **Geography:** Applies to revenue in all territories

### Example Monthly Calculation:
- Monthly Revenue: $1,000,000
