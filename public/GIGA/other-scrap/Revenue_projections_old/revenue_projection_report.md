## KEY ASSUMPTIONS BY SCENARIO

### Conservative Scenario
- Initial licensees: 2-5 per technology
- Average licensee revenue: $300K-$600K annually
- Licensee growth: 5% per year
- Market penetration: 1%
- **Best for:** Niche applications, limited market acceptance

### Moderate Scenario
- Initial licensees: 3-12 per technology
- Average licensee revenue: $1.2M-$2.5M annually
- Licensee growth: 12% per year
- Market penetration: 5%
- **Best for:** Growing adoption in specific industries

### Optimistic Scenario
- Initial licensees: 8-25 per technology
- Average licensee revenue: $3.5M-$8M annually
- Licensee growth: 20% per year
- Market penetration: 15%
- **Best for:** Strong market validation and multi-industry adoption

### Breakthrough Scenario
- Initial licensees: 15-50 per technology
- Average licensee revenue: $10M-$25M annually
- Licensee growth: 35% per year (first 10 years), 15% thereafter
- Market penetration: 30%
- **Best for:** Revolutionary technology with widespread commercial application

## RISK FACTORS

### High Risk:
- **No patent protection** - IP licensing without patents is vulnerable
- **Technology validation** - Claims require extraordinary proof
- **Competition** - Alternative technologies may emerge
- **Enforcement** - Difficulty monitoring and collecting royalties

### Medium Risk:
- **Market adoption** - New technologies face adoption barriers
- **Regulatory** - Advanced propulsion/computing may face restrictions
- **Economic cycles** - 20-year horizon spans multiple recessions

### Low Risk:
- **Licensee diversification** - Multiple technologies reduce single-point failure
- **15% royalty rate** - Reasonable and competitive rate

## BOTTOM LINE

**Most Likely Outcome:** Conservative to Moderate ($43M - $1B)  
**Best Case (with proof):** Optimistic ($16.7B)  
**Transformational Case:** Breakthrough ($72B) - requires revolutionary validation

**Key Decision Point:** The difference between scenarios depends almost entirely on:
1. **Technology validation and proof of concept**
2. **Patent/IP protection strength**
3. **Initial market traction (first 10-20 licensees)**

Without demonstrated technology and strong IP protection, even the Conservative scenario may be optimistic.
